# Own the Work

Keep one clear owner for the outcome and an explicit next action.

## Apply

- Identify who carries the work to completion and who can resolve consequential decisions. One person or agent can hold several roles on a small task.
- Keep active work small enough to finish and verify. Prioritize removing blockers before starting additional unrelated work.
- State a blocker with the missing fact or decision, its effect, and who can resolve it. Continue work that does not depend on that answer.
- Delegate only when permitted and useful. Give each delegate bounded scope, current instructions, required evidence, and a clear handoff; avoid overlapping writes to shared artifacts.
- Inspect delegated results before integrating them. The owner remains responsible for the result and its evidence.
- At handoff, provide the current scope and artifact, completed checks, unresolved gaps, and the next action. Include later user corrections.

## Evidence

The existing task record should make the current owner, next action, and blockers clear. A handoff should let the recipient continue without reconstructing the conversation.

## Boundaries

This principle does not require multiple agents, a new queue system, or a separate status document. Ownership changes do not expand the task's scope or permissions.
