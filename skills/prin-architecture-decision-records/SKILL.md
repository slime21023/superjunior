# prin-architecture-decision-records

## When to apply

Capture or evaluate a consequential architecture decision with lasting constraints or trade-offs.

## Principles

Record the decision's context, chosen option, rationale, and consequences. Keep proposed choices distinct from accepted decisions and implementation status.

## Application notes

Use existing ADR conventions and link to the source of approval and relevant earlier records. If a decision supersedes another, preserve the historical rationale and link the two records.

See the [worked example](references/examples.md). See the [source guidance](https://cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions).
