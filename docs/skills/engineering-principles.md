Use this skill when analyzing, implementing, debugging, refactoring, or reviewing code. Apply the principles relevant to the task: understand context, keep changes minimal, express intent clearly, preserve meaningful boundaries, and verify observable outcomes.

Findings or decisions should include supporting evidence, material risks, and any required next action. Existing project conventions and demonstrated needs determine how the principles apply.

The [four-layer guide](/skills/principles-four-layer-design/) and [Karpathy guidelines](/skills/karpathy-guidelines/) provide supporting explanations. They are handbook guides, not separate installable skills.
