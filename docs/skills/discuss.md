## Purpose

Help an unclear idea become a goal, a scope, and one direction you have confirmed, using the simplest approach that achieves the goal.

## When to use

You have an idea but not yet a clear goal, the scope keeps growing, or you must choose between directions before writing requirements or design.

## What to provide

The idea or question, and any known constraints. A rough description is enough.

## What you get

A short discussion that narrows the idea, offers at most three directions with a recommendation, and ends with the goal, scope, chosen direction, rejected alternatives, open decisions, and the skill to use next. The result stays in the conversation unless you ask for a record under `_artifacts/discuss/`.

## Use with

Give the confirmed goal and scope to [specify](/skills/specify/) to write requirements, or to [design-component](/skills/design-component/) when the structure needs design. Use [explore](/skills/explore/) first when the direction depends on evidence about the current system. See [workflow combinations](/workflows/) for the handoff.
