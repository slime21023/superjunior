## Purpose

Make the current system understandable enough to support a decision.

## When to use

You need to trace behavior, find related code or history, compare technical options, or discover project-configured checks.

## What to provide

A concrete question and any known scope, revision, or symptoms.

## What you get

Findings tied to code, history, or observations, with evidence gaps that affect the conclusion. For configured checks, expect commands, scope, and execution status when available.

## Use with

Give the confirmed behavior and unresolved questions to [specify](/skills/specify/) when they affect the intended change. See [workflow combinations](/workflows/) for the handoff.
