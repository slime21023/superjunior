## What to provide

Symptoms, expected behavior, and available logs or reproduction context.

## What to expect

The diagnosis states whether the cause is confirmed, suspected, or unresolved. It records repeatable steps, expected and actual behavior, and the observations that support or rule out likely causes. It also identifies the behavior to restore and checks to rerun, or the next check needed to narrow the cause.
