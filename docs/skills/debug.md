## Purpose

Connect the trigger and failure mechanism to the symptom with evidence.

## When to use

The behavior differs from expectations, including failures that occur intermittently.

## What to provide

The symptom, expected result, environment, and any reproduction steps or logs.

## What you get

A repeatable reproduction where possible, the cause or remaining uncertainty, and a corrective direction or next diagnostic check.

## Use with

Give the reproduction to [implement-it](/skills/implement-it/) for an authorized fix and to [test-it](/skills/test-it/) for regression verification. See [workflow combinations](/workflows/) for the handoff.
