## Purpose

Find actionable defects and risks with evidence the author can inspect.

## When to use

You have a concrete artifact or diff and want findings before accepting it.

## What to provide

The exact revision or artifact, relevant requirements, and any available verification.

## What you get

Prioritized findings with locations, impact, and ways to check the correction. A review also reports scope and material evidence gaps.

## Use with

Give the author a finding and its expected behavior for a focused [implement-it](/skills/implement-it/) change. See [workflow combinations](/workflows/) for the handoff.
