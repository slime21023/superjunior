## What to provide

The artifact or diff and any applicable requirements or standards.

## What to expect

The review leads with prioritized findings. Each finding names its location, trigger, impact, and a way to verify the correction. The review also identifies evidence gaps that affect a judgment and records the artifact, review basis, and material verification limits. The reviewed artifact stays unchanged.
