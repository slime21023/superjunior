## What to provide

The requested outcome and available constraints or context.

## What to expect

The specification is saved under `_spec/` at the project root. It records the goal, requirements and scope, and verifiable acceptance criteria. When assumptions or decisions affect the expected behavior, the specification identifies the affected requirements or criteria. The final reply links to the file and calls out decisions that block acceptance or implementation.

For example, a CSV export request can specify that export downloads a readable CSV while leaving the expected rows pending until the user chooses between the filtered list and all notes.
