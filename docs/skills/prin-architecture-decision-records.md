## When to use

Capture or evaluate a consequential architecture decision with lasting constraints or trade-offs.

## How it changes the work

Record the decision's context, chosen option, rationale, and consequences. Keep proposed choices distinct from accepted decisions and implementation status.

A team chooses a queue over synchronous notification delivery to tolerate temporary provider outages. Record the resulting eventual delivery, added operational ownership, and the status of implementation separately.
